Operation Nightjar

A night raid in the snow. One doctor who won't carry a gun, one ledger, and three tanks of nerve agent.

Briefing: Nightjar

Agent: Dr. Thomas Hale, physician and chemist. Escort: Eli Adler.

Situation: The Kesselwald works is making a nerve agent in quantity. London believes it is meant for the invasion beaches.

Orders: Enter through the south wire. Take the formula ledger from the records office. Set one charge on each of the three storage tanks. Get back to Adler at the treeline before the timers run out.

Note: Dr. Hale refuses to carry a weapon. He can subdue a guard from behind. He will not kill one.
